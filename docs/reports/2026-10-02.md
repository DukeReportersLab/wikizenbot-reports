- **Distinguish urgency types**: the Fiber/airport/Lazarbeem ANI threads are "fast-boiling" (many comments within hours, real sanction risk soon), while the AI-wikitext and Image-Carousel-close RfCs are "slow-burn but consequential" (days-long, wide participation, will set lasting policy/precedent). Both deserve attention, but on different clocks.
