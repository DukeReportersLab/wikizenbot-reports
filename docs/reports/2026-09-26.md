## 📜 Slower but consequential policy discussions (the "AI content" cluster)

Several threads across different venues are converging on the same unresolved question — **who bears responsibility for identifying and fixing suspected AI-generated content** — suggesting this is the live fault line in Wikipedia governance right now:

- **[Onus for maintenance templates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AVillage_pump_%28policy%29#Onus_for_maintenance_templates)** (VPP, 67 comments, updated 15h ago) — directly asks whether taggers must explain/fix AI-flagged content or whether the burden falls on other editors.
- **[RfC: Permitting or prohibiting AI in creating/formatting Wikitext](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AVillage_pump_%28policy%29#RfC%3A_Permitting_or_prohibiting_AI_in_creating/formatting_Wikitext)** (VPP, 58 comments/30 participants, updated 11.5h ago) — a formal RfC that will set binding policy.
- **[Backlog of expired proposed deletions for LLM content](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard#Backlog_of_expired_proposed_deletions_for_LLM_content)** (AN) — an operational symptom of the same underlying disagreement.

Worth watching as a set rather than individually — the AN/I conduct case above (#1) is essentially what happens when this policy gap isn't resolved.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 The "Newcomer Tasks" cluster — large, sprawling, genuinely important

- **[Newcomer tasks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AVillage_pump_%28miscellaneous%29#Newcomer_tasks)** (VP Misc) — **275 comments, 196K characters**, the single largest thread scanned, still active (8h since last comment). Extensive first-hand testimony from new editors describing the Growth-team "suggested edits" feature as actively counterproductive — pointing them at articles they can't understand and generating cleanup work rather than reducing it.
- **[Can the "link suggestions" newcomer task be disabled for mathematical articles?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AVillage_pump_%28proposals%29#Can_the_%22link_suggestions%22_newcomer_task_be_disabled_for_mathematical_articles%3F)** (VPR) — 133 comments, 118K characters, a domain-specific spinoff of the same complaint, explicitly cross-linked to the thread above.

Together these represent a substantial, editor-driven push to overhaul or gut a core WMF-built onboarding feature. Slow-burn rather than urgent-urgent, but high consequence if it results in a feature change — worth a human editor's eyes even though nothing will resolve in the next 24 hours.
