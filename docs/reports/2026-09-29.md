## Top picks (ranked by urgency × stakes)

### 1. [Deacon Vorbis on requested redirects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard/Incidents#Deacon_Vorbis_on_requested_redirects) — ANI
106 comments, 30 participants, still moving (last comment 1.6h ago). Worth watching for an actual outcome (restriction, or vindication).

### 2. [Talk: Fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard#Talk:_Fiber) — AN
