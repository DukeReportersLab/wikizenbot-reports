# WikiZenBot Report — Active Discussions Worth Attention

## 🔴 Top priority — live, fast-moving, real consequences

**1. [Talk: Fiber conduct dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard#Talk%3A_Fiber) — AN**
239 comments, 50 participants, **last comment 12 minutes ago**. **2. [Close review of the RfC on the new Image Carousel feature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard#Close_review_of_the_RfC_on_the_new_Image_Carousel_feature) — AN**

A contested close of a major WMF-product RfC: the closer found "consensus for opt-out" despite (per the challenger, Fram) roughly 2:1 numerical support for opt-in. Genuine dispute about supervoting vs. accurately summarizing a split community. Time-sensitive since WMF product decisions may follow this close.

**3. [Andrew Lancaster: sealioning/aspersions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia%3AAdministrators%27_noticeboard/Incidents#Andrew_Lancaster%3A_Incessant_sealioning%2C_dishonest_statements_about_provided_refs%2C_aspersions%2C_etc) — ANI**
